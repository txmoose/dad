Dad passed yesterday around 5 in the morning. It feels strange. I'm sad, but not only sad. He's in a better place now, and he's at peace. I love you, dad. With all my heart, and I'll miss you every day of my life.

We're going to have a small viewing and memorial for him on Tuesday. Any and all are welcome, if you can make it. The obituary says a donation to the Beaumont Humaine Society is preferred, but if you feels so inclined, Mom would love a small plant of some kind she can keep and nurture as opposed to flowers.

I'm going to leave this site up, but this will be the last update to it. Probably. Thank you to everyone who has followed it. I know it seems more like a journal for myself, but I thrive on providing something of value to those who are important to me, and just knowing that people were following this on their own means more to me than I could really express to you all. So from the bottom of my heart, thank you. It really does help me in this time knowing that this small effort was useful.
